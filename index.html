The Problem

Modern finances are broken,
and it's not your fault.

The problem isn't literacy. It's that no tool brings it all together.

01

Fragmented Access

Traditional financial services are siloed, forcing customers to juggle multiple providers, accounts, and interfaces.

02

Overwhelm

Only 37% of US households can handle basic budgeting and across Europe, just 18% have strong financial skills overall.

03

Avoidance

Financial anxiety doubles the likelihood of ignoring balances entirely — making the problem worse over time.

The Solution

One place for everything.
Your accounts, your wealth, your future.

⚡

Automated account aggregation

Automatically aggregates bank accounts, cards, and brokerages via open banking (powered by Plaid & TrueLayer).

📊

Live net worth tracking

Net worth tracking alongside spending habits and investment performance — updated in real time.

🤖

AI-powered spending analysis

Personalised suggestions based on your spending habits to reach your goals, tackling both the knowledge gap and the anxiety around money.

🌍

The only tracker that gets multi-currency right

Full multi-currency support with historical FX rates applied retroactively — so your portfolio performance is accurate at any point in time, not just today.
