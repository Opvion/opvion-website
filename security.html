Security

Your data is safe with us.

Bank-grade security, EU data residency, and zero data selling.

Encryption
🛡️
AES-256 Encryption at Rest

All your financial data is encrypted at rest using AES-256, the same standard used by banks and governments worldwide.

🔐
TLS 1.3 in Transit

Every connection between your device and our servers is protected by TLS 1.3, ensuring no data can be intercepted in transit.

🗝️
Credential Isolation

The tokens we use to fetch your bank data are stored in a separate, access-controlled vault, not alongside your account information.

Data & Privacy
🏳️
Data Stays in the European Union

All servers are hosted in EU member states. Your data never leaves the EU and is subject to the strictest privacy laws in the world.

📜
GDPR Compliant

We are fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation. You have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time.

🚫
Zero Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties, ever. Our business model is subscriptions, not your data.

Banking Integrations
🔗
Read-Only Bank Access

Opvion only ever requests read-only access to your accounts. We cannot initiate payments, move funds, or make any changes on your behalf.

✅
Regulated Providers

Bank connections are powered by regulated open banking partners operating under strict open-banking laws.

🔄
Re-authentication Required

Connections to your bank expire regularly and require your explicit re-approval, giving you full control over what Opvion has access to at all times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know about how we handle your data and keep you safe.

What data do you collect and store?

We collect only what's needed to show you your financial picture. This comes either from your manual input or your read-only bank connections, which are powered by our open banking partners. We can never move or modify your money. We never store your bank login credentials.

How is my data protected?

We're based in the European Union, which means we're held to GDPR, the strictest data privacy regulation in the world. Our open banking partners use encrypted connections (TLS) and tokenization to protect data in transit, and all stored data is encrypted with AES-256. Your data is stored exclusively on EU servers, and we never sell it to anyone. We make our money through subscriptions, not by selling your data.

Can I execute transactions through Opvion?

No, and that's by design. We connect to your bank with read-only access, which means it is impossible to move or modify your money through our app for anyone, including our own team. Even if someone gained access to your Opvion account, there is simply no mechanism to initiate any transaction.

How does Opvion make money?

Through subscriptions, not your data. We offer several plans to suit different needs. You can view all plans and pricing here.

Can I delete my account and all my data?

Yes. You can delete your account at any time directly from your account settings. The process is straightforward and permanently removes your account along with all associated data from our systems. We'd be sorry to see you go, but we make it easy.
