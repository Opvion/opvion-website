08 · Roadmap Customer-pull

What we're scoping next, with your input.

The items below are unbuilt and on the radar. If any of them are blockers for your firm, tell us and we'll scope them directly against your requirements. For this buyer, that conversation is a feature, not a workaround.

📋 IBOR-Lite Roadmap

Investment book of record: transaction-level NAV computed daily, with full audit trail and time-series export. Bridges the gap between a broker statement and a proper fund accounting system.

📝 Regulatory Templates Roadmap

Pre-structured outputs for ADV Part 2, Form PF, AIFMD Annex IV, 13F, and GIPS composite construction. Tell us which jurisdiction you file in and we'll scope the field mapping.

⚡ Execution Analytics Roadmap

TCA-style trade cost analysis: implementation shortfall, market impact, and slippage versus arrival price and VWAP benchmarks. Requires execution venue data feed.

🚫 Compliance & Restricted Lists Roadmap

Pre-trade compliance checks against firm-level restricted lists, sector exclusions, and ESG screens. Real-time block on any proposed trade that violates a rule.

📐 Advanced Quant Roadmap

Factor exposure (Fama-French, Barra-style), VaR and CVaR at position and portfolio level, tail-risk analytics, and drawdown path analysis with conditional overlays.

Tell us what you need.

If any item above is a hard requirement, contact us and we'll scope it against your specific setup: timeline, data sources, and output format included.
